Trees have been around for ~400 million years. There are currently ~3 trillion mature trees on earth. (Thanks, Wikipedia!) Modern humans have been around for hundreds of thousands of years. Primates have been around for millions of years. (Thanks, again, Wikipedia!)

What I'm getting at here is that trees and humans have been around each other for a long time, yeah?

So how is it that my allergies are so bad? How is it possible that millions of years of evolution have produced me, a walking talking ape that is so allergic to trees that I need four - FOUR! - allergy shots a month?

I mean, come on, body. Are you even, like, trying?

The good news? My doc popped into the room today to make sure I, you know, hadn't DIED or anything after having multiple vials of poison shot directly into my body and told me how much she was enjoying View Terry Pratchett's website - Terry Pratchett.

I got my doc hooked on Terry Pratchett books, y'all!

She said she was reading one at home and listening to a second one in her car. She couldn't stop smiling.

Your friendly neighborhood librarian, risking death by anaphylaxis to get the right book in the right hand.
