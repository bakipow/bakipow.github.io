Broke library law today.

Older couple vacationing from out of state. He's double fisting bags of books he bought at the Friends of the Library book sale. She's clutching three library books to her bosom asking if she can check them out.

Let her know if she's willing to fib she can get a temp card. Only two books checked out at a time though.

I lean in.

She leans in.

Hubby looks worried.

If you tell the circ desk that you just moved here and want a card . . .

She nods.

. . . show 'em your license. Tell them no you don't have any proof of local address. Make one up. Give 'em your hotel's address.

And? She asks.

That's it. Dem's da rules.

Is it okay, she asks.

Reads reference librarian right here on the badge, I say.

Give me an address, she demands.

Ah, I say, tapping my nose. Make sure I'm implicated if it all goes south. Nicely played.

Hubby throws up his hands (holding the book bags) and states that he's out. Goes to sit down nearby.

Give her the address. If you hear a siren or see flashing lights, I add as she's on her way, run.

Hubby and I eavesdrop as the circ staff grill her. She's gonna fold, says the hubby. Naw, I reply, your girl's going the distance.

She says the magic phrase: temp card.

See, I say to hubby, it's all good.

She comes back with the books and a circ receipt. Congratulations all around. They ask me why. I state that I'd rather see books out the library being read rather than molding away in the stacks.
