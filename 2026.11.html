For example, if someone comes in wearing a tee reading "Hitler Rulez" on one side and "Death2NonWhites" on the other? We're kicking them out until they wear more appropriate clothing.

Library pro-tip: If you're making patrons uneasy because you're calling for their genocide, well, that tends to go against library policy.

On the other hand, simply because a patron is offended doesn't mean we're taking action.

If, for instance, you're using the patron computers and occasionally swearing under your breath because working with Microsoft Word is frustrating you to no end, and another patron, also at the patron computers, takes offense at such language and brings it to the attention of staff? We're not gonna do much about it. Cursing quietly at Microsoft is not against library policy. We will ask the offended patron if they want to move to another computer so they're out of aural range, but that's about it.

Unfortunately, in the real life example of the issue above, the offended patron thought they shouldn't have to move and asked to speak to the director.
